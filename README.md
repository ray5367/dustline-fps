Dustline is a round-based tactical FPS in the Cinder Yard: you and four Vulture bots attack against five Jackal bots, first to 5 round wins. Plant the charge at Site A or Site B (hold E), or wipe the enemy squad out. Money earned each round buys rifles, a sniper, a shotgun, an SMG, armor and frag grenades in the buy phase. Gunplay rewards stopping to aim: hitscan shots with headshot bonuses, recoil and spread that punish firing on the move, acceleration-based movement, crouching and map collision. Bots follow waypoint routes to the sites, hold positions, react to sight and to the sound of gunfire, and converge to defuse a planted charge. All art is original geometry in a desert-industrial palette and all sounds are synthesized with WebAudio — no assets, no network requests, just static files and three.js 0.187.0.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — click to play. `W A S D` move, mouse look, click fire, `R` reload, `1`/`2` weapons, `Ctrl` crouch, `E` plant (hold), `B` buy menu, `G` throw frag, right mouse aim.
